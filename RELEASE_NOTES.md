**English** · Download **`ChestCounter.exe`** below and run it. Nothing to install.

> **Turn on replays in Fortnite first**: *Settings → Game → Replays → Record Replays: On*. The tool only reads these replay files: no game modification, no access to the game's memory.

**What's new in 1.0.3**
- Chests are now counted from Fortnite's match replays instead of reading the screen: no setup, no prompt to calibrate, and the replays still on your PC are counted right away
- In-game overlay that blends into the HUD: your total, your last match and this session
- A window with your full history, a report of all your statistics, the treasure hunt and your chances
- English, French and Spanish

This version is based on [LED-N/Fortnite-ChestCounter](https://github.com/LED-N/Fortnite-ChestCounter) (MIT). Updates are checked on this repository.
